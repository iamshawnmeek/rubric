How it works

Three steps from blank page to clear feedback.

  1. 01

    Build the rubric once

    Objectives, groups, weights and a grading scale. Keep it Simple with a percentage per objective, or go Detailed with performance levels and descriptors. Or start from a ready-made template.

  2. 02

    Grade the whole class, fast

    Tap a level or a score for each objective and the grade updates live. Drop in comments from your comment bank, apply late penalties, and mark work excused or missing without breaking stride.

  3. 03

    See what's landing

    A gradebook across every assignment, score distributions, objective mastery, and a short list of students who need attention, so the next lesson starts where the class actually is.

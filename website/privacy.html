Privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

The short version

  • Rubric works fully offline. Everything you create is stored on your device.
  • If you choose to create an account, your data is also stored on Rubric's server so your devices stay in sync. Only your account can access it.
  • We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or third-party trackers, and we never sell or share your data or your students' data.

What Rubric stores

The things you create in the app: classes, student names and the details you add (such as student numbers, emails or notes), rubrics, assignments, grades, feedback and your comment bank.

Without an account, all of this stays on your device. It's included in backups you choose to export, and it's removed when you erase the app's data or delete the app.

If you create an account

Sync is optional. When you sign in, Rubric stores your email address, a securely hashed password (never the password itself), and a copy of your data on its server, so it can sync between your devices.

  • Every record is tied to your account, and the server only lets your account read or change it.
  • Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • The server is hosted in the United States (Oracle Cloud, Chicago). Encrypted backups of the database are kept for 14 days.
  • Signing out removes your account's data from that device. It stays on the server so you can sign in again.

Students

Rubric is a tool for teachers. Students don't use it or sign in to it. Student information is entered by the teacher, belongs to the teacher's school, and is used only to provide the app's features to that teacher. We never use it for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

This website

yourrubric.com sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from third parties. Our web server keeps standard access logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) for a short time to keep the service running and secure.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything on a device from Settings → Erase all data. To delete your account, choose Settings → Delete account in the app: your account and all of its synced data are removed from the server right away. Copies in the server's backups age out within 14 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
